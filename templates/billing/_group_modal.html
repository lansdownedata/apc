{% comment %} billing/_group_modal.html — add a group under this account, or edit one. A group is who an invoice is made out to, and a sub-customer in QuickBooks. Its terms are optional: blank means "whatever the account says", which is why the picker's empty option names the account's own terms rather than reading "None". Which group is the default is not edited here — that is a deliberate, separate choice on the row itself, so a rename can never quietly move it. One x-show on the OUTER container and no x-transition — see _account_modal.html for the bug that rule exists for. Driven by billingCard() in static/js/app.js. Requires `billing_account` and `billing_terms`. {% endcomment %}
