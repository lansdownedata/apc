Affiliate scoring

Every active affiliate gets a reliability score out of 100 on Vendors → Performance and on their own page. This is how it is worked out. These rules can't be edited here; ask your developer to change them.

How the parts combine

  • Each part is scored from 0 to 100 on a straight line between its zero and its full marks. Halfway between the two is 50.
  • The score is the weighted average of the parts, using the percentages above.
  • A part with nothing to measure yet (no reviews, no driver-info times) is left out, and the others are re-weighted to fill the gap. It never counts as zero.
  • An affiliate needs {{ min_trips }} confirmed trips in the window before a score shows. Below that the page says "Not enough trips" and still shows the raw numbers.
  • On the ranking, a part scoring under {{ weak_below }} is called out as a weak spot. The two lowest are shown.

Which trips count

  • Trips are counted by pickup date, in the trip's own timezone. The windows are {{ windows|join:", " }}; an affiliate's own page uses {{ default_window|lower }}.
  • Only farmed-out trips the affiliate confirmed count. In-house trips and inactive affiliates are left out.
  • A cancellation counts against the affiliate who last held the trip when it was marked Cancelled by Affiliate.
  • Pricing compares each payout to the median payout across all affiliates for that vehicle type in the same window, so it moves as other affiliates' prices move.
  • Medians are used for the two timing parts, so one very slow trip doesn't sink the number.
