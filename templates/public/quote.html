Terms & Conditions

This document contains the terms and conditions between All Pro Charter, Inc. (the service provider) and client. This document, when signed by the client, will constitute a legally binding contract. This contract shall be deemed to be fully in force if the customer has made any payments to secure the dates or to reserve the equipment. You are therefore requested to carefully read the entire contract document before you sign it. If you find any errors or omissions, you should return the contract unsigned and make annotation of all errors or omissions on this document. If the service provider agrees that the original document is in error, a new document will be prepared and sent to the client.

Itinerary

Driver will be furnished a copy of the work order and itinerary, and he/she will be instructed to strictly follow it. The driver has no authority to make changes to the trip schedule without prior approval from service provider and contracted client. If agreed upon, client shall be responsible for all additional hourly charges plus any additional fees that may be incurred.

All Itineraries are subject to review and approval by service provider prior to departure and must comply with local, state, and federal law. The Department of Transportation prohibits drivers to be on duty for more than 15 hours per day, 10 of which are driving hours. The driver must be given 8 consecutive hours off duty before returning to "on duty".

Service

It is understood and agreed that the performance of the service detailed in this work order is subject to tariff regulations and is contingent upon the service provider's ability to furnish the equipment and perform the service. The service provider reserves the right to lease equipment from other service providers in order to fulfill this charter agreement.

Additional Charges

Client is responsible for all agreed upon fees, including but not limited to tolls, parking, park entrance fees, driver lodging and/or attractions. Groups are to provide each driver with a single, nonsmoking room (when applicable).

Payments

50% deposit is required upon booking to secure the reservation. Remaining balance due 30 days prior to the service. If reservation is made within 30 days of the reservation full payment is due upon booking.

Cancellation Policy

Sedans/SUVs must be cancelled 72 hours prior to service date.

Luxury Vans must be cancelled 14 days prior to service date.

Buses, Stretch Limos, and Speciality Vehicles must be cancelled 30 days prior to the service.

Liability

The service provider does not guarantee to arrive or depart from any point at a specific time but will endeavor to maintain the schedule submitted by its agent, employee or the customer. Service provider guarantees that they will do everything within reason to ensure that passengers are transported per the customer's itinerary. Additionally, the service provider shall not be responsible for any damages that result from the cancellation or delay.

Repairs or Damage

It is understood and agreed upon that the customer as well as passengers are liable for all beyond normal repairs or cleaning to the charged vehicle caused by the chartering party's passengers. It is also understood that the chartering party is liable for any damages caused by the chartering party's passengers, to the equipment, employees or visited location during the duration of the charter or as a result of the charter.

Motor Coach Equipment and Accessories

Accessories such as video equipment, restrooms and stereos are for the use of the passengers and while the service provider will endeavor to maintain this equipment, the service provider will not guarantee its availability or operation at any point during the charter. Service provider reserves the right to refuse to transport a person or persons under the influence or whose conduct is objectionable to other passengers or the safe operation of the coach. Smoking is prohibited while on the coach. These conditions are designed to achieve maximum safety and comfort for customer and operator.
